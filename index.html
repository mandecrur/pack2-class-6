Casuísticas de práctica
6
During his explanation of there is / there are, Ricardo notices that one of his students has been drawing in his notebook for several minutes and is not looking at the board. Which auxiliary process of learning is failing in this student?
A
Transfer, because the student cannot apply the knowledge to a new context.
B
Sustained attention, because the student is unable to keep his mental resources focused on the stimulus over a prolonged period.
C
Retrieval, because the student cannot recall information stored in his long-term memory.
La atención sostenida es la capacidad de mantener el foco en un estímulo durante un período prolongado (Posner y Petersen, 1990). El alumno que dibuja ha desviado sus recursos mentales hacia otra actividad, lo que impide que el input lingüístico del docente se procese y transforme en conocimiento. La transferencia y la recuperación son procesos que ocurren después de que el aprendizaje ha tenido lugar, no durante la recepción inicial del estímulo.
7
Verónica proposes the following to her students: "We are going to record a podcast in English about the best places to visit in our region. It will be uploaded to the school website so other students and parents can listen to it." Several students start participating enthusiastically without being asked. Which type of motivation predominates and why does MINEDU prioritise it?
A
Extrinsic motivation, because the task has a final product that will be published and seen by others.
B
Intrinsic motivation, because the authentic challenge generates inherent satisfaction in the activity and produces greater engagement and sustained learning than external rewards.
C
Regulated amotivation, because students participate without fully understanding the purpose of the task.
Ryan y Deci (2000) definen la motivación intrínseca como la impulsada por la satisfacción inherente a la propia actividad. El MINEDU la prioriza porque produce mayor engagement y aprendizaje sostenido en comparación con los incentivos externos. Plantear retos auténticos con sentido real —como un podcast para la comunidad escolar— activa este tipo de motivación al dar un propósito comunicativo genuino a la tarea.
8
Daniel tells his students that whoever gets the highest score on the vocabulary test on Friday will receive a prize. Most students study hard all week. Which statement best describes this situation?
A
It is an example of intrinsic motivation, because students feel genuinely interested in learning vocabulary.
B
It is an example of extrinsic motivation, and although it may generate short-term effort, it produces less sustained learning than authentic challenges aligned with MINEDU's approach.
C
It is an example of metacognitive planning, because students organise their study time in advance.
La motivación extrínseca (Ryan y Deci, 2000) está impulsada por recompensas o consecuencias externas —en este caso, el premio. Aunque puede generar esfuerzo a corto plazo, el MINEDU prioriza la motivación intrínseca porque produce aprendizaje más profundo y duradero. Organizar el tiempo de estudio es una estrategia de planificación, pero no convierte la situación en metacognición.
9
At the beginning of a lesson on present continuous, Lorena asks: "Without looking at your notes, can you remember three action verbs we studied last week? Try to say them aloud." What auxiliary learning process is Lorena activating?
A
Scaffolding, because Lorena provides verbal support to help students access the new content.
B
Retrieval, because students must evoke information stored in their long-term memory without any external aid.
C
Near transfer, because students apply previously learned verbs in a new grammatical context.
La recuperación (retrieval, Tulving y Thomson, 1973) es la capacidad de evocar información almacenada en la memoria a largo plazo. Pedir a los alumnos que recuerden verbos sin apuntes es una práctica de retrieval activo que refuerza las trazas de memoria. No es andamiaje porque Lorena no provee soporte adicional, y no es transferencia porque no se aplica el conocimiento a un contexto nuevo: simplemente se evoca.
10
Kevin studied a list of irregular verbs silently at home the night before. The next day, during an oral storytelling game, he cannot remember the past form of "break." What principle best explains Kevin's difficulty?
A
The principle of encoding specificity (Tulving & Thomson, 1973): the retrieval conditions (spontaneous oral production) do not match the encoding conditions (silent reading), which makes recall less effective.
B
The scaffolding principle, because Kevin did not receive enough support from the teacher during the encoding phase.
C
The transfer principle, because Kevin studied the verbs in isolation and cannot apply them in a real communicative context.
El principio de especificidad de codificación (Tulving y Thomson, 1973) establece que la recuperación es más efectiva cuando el contexto de recuperación coincide con el de codificación. Kevin estudió leyendo en silencio (condición de codificación) pero debe recordar en producción oral espontánea (condición de recuperación diferente), lo que dificulta el acceso a la información. No es un problema de andamiaje ni de transferencia propiamente dicha.
